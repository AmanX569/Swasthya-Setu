Visual First-Aid Offline Guides · प्राथमिक उपचार

🐍

Snakebite (सर्पदंश)

DO: Keep patient still and calm. Immobilize the limb with a splint. Rush to PHC for anti-venom.
DON'T: Do NOT cut, suck, tie tight tourniquets or apply ice.

❤️

CPR & Cardiac (हार्ट अटैक)

DO: If unresponsive and not breathing, place hands in center of chest. Push hard and fast (100–120/min).
CALL: 108 ambulance immediately.

☀️

Heatstroke (लू लगना)

DO: Move to shade. Sponge with cool water. Give ORS / lemonade in small sips if conscious.
DON'T: Do not give ice-cold water or heavy food.
