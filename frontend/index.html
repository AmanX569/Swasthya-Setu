Swasthya Setu User Guide & Help Center

Role Tutorials, Step-by-Step Instructions & FAQs

🌾 Citizen & Patient Quickstart Guide

  • Self-Registration: Click "New User? Register" on the login screen. Fill in your details to automatically receive your 14-digit ABHA Card.
  • Request Teleconsultation: Click "+ Request Doctor Consultation", select your preferred registered doctor, type your symptoms, and send the request.
  • Download e-Prescriptions: Once the doctor prescribes medications, you will hear a notification chime. Click "📥 Download PDF" on your prescription card to download your official ABDM PDF.
  • Delete Prescriptions: Click "🗑️ Delete" after downloading to keep your health locker clean and save storage.
  • Emergency 108 SOS: Click "🚨 108 SOS" or "📍 GPS SOS" for instant emergency ambulance dispatch.

🩺 Medical Officer / Doctor Quickstart Guide

  • Manage OPD Queue: The queue displays live walk-in patients, citizen consultation requests, and ASHA referrals with triage badges.
  • Conduct Consultation: Click "Consult & Prescribe" on any patient in the queue. Select Jan Aushadhi generic medicines, enter advice, and submit.
  • Instant Queue Removal: Upon prescription issuance, the patient is removed from the active queue and saved in your issued prescription history.
  • Dynamic PDF Export: Prescriptions carry your name, medical registration number, and digital signature for ABDM compliance.

🤝 ASHA & ANM Frontline Field Worker Guide

  • Register & Refer Patients: Click "+ Register & Refer Patient to Doctor" to create an ABHA card and dispatch a referral directly to a registered doctor.
  • Maternal ANC Care: Register pregnant mothers, record gestational weeks, BP, Hb levels, and track high-risk pregnancies.
  • Child UIP Immunization: Track vaccine doses (BCG, Pentavalent, Rotavirus, MR) and upcoming due dates.
  • Master Field Registry: View all referrals, ANC records, vaccines, and home visit logs in a consolidated master table.

👑 District Health Administrator Guide

  • Provision Staff: Click "+ Provision New Staff" to create Doctors, ASHA workers, or other Admins with temporary credentials.
  • Live Staff Removal: Click "✕ Remove" on any staff row to immediately delete from local memory and Supabase Cloud.
  • Hospital Bed Allocation: Adjust general, ICU, and oxygen bed counts in real time.
  • Blood Bank Stock: Manage blood unit inventory across all 8 blood groups.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I create or find my 14-digit ABHA Card? ▼
When you register as a Citizen / Patient, an official 14-digit Ayushman Bharat ABHA card is generated automatically. You can print or download your ABHA card from the Patient Hub.
Can I log in using my mobile number? ▼
Yes! All roles (Citizens, Doctors, ASHA workers, and Admins) can log in using their 10-digit registered mobile number along with their private password. If you have multiple roles, a role selection menu will appear.
How does live cloud synchronization work? ▼
Swasthya Setu is connected directly to Supabase PostgreSQL with real-time WebSockets. Any prescription issued, bed updated, or referral sent syncs instantly across all devices.
